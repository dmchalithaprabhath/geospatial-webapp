Route logic

The loop groups Sigiriya, Pidurangala and Minneriya together first, then uses Kandy as the gateway to the scenic hill-country train before finishing on the south coast.

🛕

Cultural Triangle first

Go straight to Sigiriya so the next morning can be used for Pidurangala sunrise and Sigiriya Rock.

🐘

Minneriya safari

The safari fits naturally from Sigiriya/Habarana as an afternoon jeep experience.

🚆

Tea train by daylight

Use the Kandy-Ella line during the day for tea estates, valleys and mountain views.

🌊

Ocean train finish

Return to Colombo and use the southbound coastal railway for Hikkaduwa and Galle.

Practical notes

Verify final train times, train seats, safari permits, weather and exact flight timing before confirming hotels.

Train seats

Reserve Kandy-Ella seats early where possible. A shorter Nanu Oya-Ella ride is a backup if full-route seats are not available.

Safari timing

Use a licensed jeep. Afternoon is usually the easier safari window after the morning rock climbs.

Departure buffer

For an early Sep 7 flight, stay near Negombo or the airport on Sep 6. For a late flight, the south coast can still work.
